INVENTA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

INVENTA ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app may process and how it is used.

Information collected

INVENTA may process invention prompts, generated text, uploaded images, user preferences, and locally stored invention projects that you create in the app. If optional cloud services are configured, account information and synchronized project data may be processed by those services.

How information is used

Information is used to generate inventions, provide app features, save projects, synchronize data when enabled, respond to support requests, and maintain or improve the service. AI providers may process prompts or images only when a cloud generation feature is used.

Permissions

The app may request camera and microphone access for features that use those inputs. You can grant, deny, or revoke device permissions in your device settings. Internet access is used to connect to optional cloud and AI services.

Data storage and retention

Projects and preferences are stored locally where supported. Cloud data is retained for as long as needed to provide synchronization and account features. You may delete local projects in the app and request deletion of cloud data from the contact below.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to host the app, store account data, or provide AI generation. Those providers process data according to their own privacy and security terms.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

INVENTA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You may stop using the app, delete local content, disable optional cloud features, revoke device permissions, or request access to or deletion of data where applicable.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests may be sent to the developer contact shown on the application's official store listing.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will indicate the latest revision.
